For financiers

Every feature protects the asset.

We designed the BMS, the VCTU and AmperOps together. Each part adds life, safety or control to the battery.

  1. Cell-level BMSSets charge and discharge limits for each cell from live pack data.5-year first life. The industry norm is 1–2 years.
  2. Humidity and heat sensingFinds moisture and high temperatures inside the pack early.Faults are found before they cause damage.
  3. Crash detectionThe VCTU accelerometer detects crashes, accidents and drops, and reports each event to the fleet owner.Fleet owners get an alert and a clear record of every impact.
  4. Bluetooth LEThe pack talks to the driver app and the swap station, also without a mobile signal.Riders see their battery in real time, and every swap and pack check is recorded at the pack.
  5. GPS and 4GEach pack reports its location and condition while the bike rides.Lost and stolen packs are found.
  6. Remote controlFrom AmperOps we update, limit or immobilise a pack, over the air.The asset stays under our control, wherever it is.
