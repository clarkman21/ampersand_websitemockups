BMS · Ampersand BMS
1%

cell-voltage accuracy. The BMS sets its current limits from charge, temperature, current and state of health, second by second.

Our own BMS keeps each pack healthy for longer.

HM1 started on a third-party BMS. In 2023 our electronics team launched the Ampersand BMS as a drop-in replacement. It is built into every MK2 and retrofitted to HM1. We control every limit and every firmware release.

  • Dynamic charge and discharge limits
  • Cell balancing
  • Over-voltage, under-voltage and over-temperature protection
  • Pre-charge soft start against inrush current
  • State of charge and state of health prediction
  • Up to 65 A regenerative braking
  • Smart under-voltage shutdown
  • OTA firmware on MK2
