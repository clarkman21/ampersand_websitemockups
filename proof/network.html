How a swap works

Three steps. No charging wait.

  1. The rider arrives. The attendant scans the empty battery in the swap app.
  2. The swap app records the charge that is left. The rider pays for the energy used (to confirm).
  3. The rider takes a full battery and leaves. The empty battery goes on charge.

The swap app also works offline, so a weak mobile signal does not stop a station.
