The system

Some say we over-engineered it. We built the system Africa deserves.

A moto-taxi rider in Kigali or Nairobi can spend more than $11 a day on fuel and lease costs and take home as little as $1.60. Petrol is the largest cost in their working life.

We replace petrol with a service. The rider swaps an empty battery for a full one and keeps working. We own each part of that service, so we control its cost, its uptime and its safety.

Software

AmperOps, the rider app and the swap app

AmperOps is our fleet and battery platform. It gives operators geofenced alarms, predictive maintenance and return on investment by station, rider and battery. The rider app shows battery level and the nearest station. The swap app runs every station, also when offline.
