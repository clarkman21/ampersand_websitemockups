AMP batteries

The battery is the core of the system. It is designed in-house, uses lithium iron phosphate (LFP) chemistry and reports its health continuously.

In service

AMP 01 · HM1.9

The pack that built the network. 350,000 km lifetime range. UN38.3 certified.

In service

AMP 02 · MK2

48 Ah pack, launched with the MK2 platform in October 2025. About 75 km per swap.

In development

AMP XL

More range per swap. Designed to work with the stations and packs already on the network.

  • BLE, GSM and GPS built in
  • Anti-theft and anti-tamper
  • Passive cooling
  • Over-the-air updates
  • Field-maintainable
  • Assembled in Rwanda

AMP 01 / 02 names follow the brand book naming (AMP_01, AMP_02, AMP_XL). The mapping to HM1.9 and MK2 is to confirm.
